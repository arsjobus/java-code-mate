# Quick Rules

These are the distilled rules that should be consulted before significant product or architectural decisions.

1. **Local before cloud.**
2. **User control before automation.**
3. **Explicit before ambient.**
4. **Interoperable before proprietary.**
5. **Simple before clever.**
6. **Understandable before abstract.**
7. **Offline before online.**
8. **Privacy before telemetry.**
9. **Human approval before AI modification.**
10. **The editor is a tool, not a destination.**
11. **Dependencies must earn their place.**
12. **A project must remain usable without the editor.**
13. **No engagement mechanics.**
14. **No mandatory account.**
15. **No silent network activity.**
